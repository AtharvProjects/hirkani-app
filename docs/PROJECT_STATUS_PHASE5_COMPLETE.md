## PHASE 5 NEW CAPABILITIES

### 🤖 AI Explanation Engine
**AIExplanationEngine (800 lines)**

**Explanation Types:**
1. **Food Summary** - Overall suitability assessment
2. **Concern Detail** - Deep dive into specific concerns
3. **Benefit Detail** - Deep dive into specific benefits
4. **Daily Summary** - Daily nutrition aggregation
5. **Weekly Summary** - Weekly trends and patterns
6. **Comparison** - Side-by-side food comparison
7. **Recommendation** - Actionable personalized guidance
8. **Educational** - General pregnancy nutrition info

**Critical Safety Features:**
- ✅ **Medical Advice Blocking** - Detects/blocks directive medical language
- ✅ **Numeric Claim Verification** - All numbers must trace to evidence
- ✅ **Source Fabrication Prevention** - No bare authority claims
- ✅ **Evidence Grounding** - Every statement backed by data
- ✅ **Hallucination Detection** - Multi-layer verification system

**Confidence Levels:**
- **High:** 2+ high-quality sources (ACOG, NIH, peer-reviewed)
- **Moderate:** 1+ high-quality source or 2+ moderate sources
- **Limited:** Minimal evidence or hallucination flags

**Tone Options:**
- Conversational (friendly, accessible)
- Clinical (professional, medical)
- Educational (teaching-focused)
- Supportive (empathetic, encouraging)

**Required Disclaimers:**
- Medical advice disclaimer (always)
- Food safety consultation reminder
- Healthcare provider guidance
- Context-specific warnings
